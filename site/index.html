Your first visit, step by step

  1. 1You call, or book online

    Tell us if you are nervous. We note it.

  2. 2A quiet welcome

    A cup of tea and a short form. No queue.

  3. 3A talk before the chair

    Face to face, sitting up, about what worries you.

  4. 4A gentle look

    We tell you what we see. Nothing happens without your yes.

  5. 5And then you go home

    With a plain plan and the next date, if you need one.
